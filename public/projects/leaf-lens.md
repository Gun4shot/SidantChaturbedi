# Leaf Lens

Concept project — sample portfolio content. Not a claim of completed work.

A little intelligence for the natural world.

A concept for identifying plants from a photograph and explaining the visual clues behind each prediction. Built around curiosity, uncertainty, and learning outdoors.

## Exploration

Compare a lightweight image classifier with a pretrained vision encoder, and show confidence alongside the prediction.
