# Pathfinder

Concept project — sample portfolio content. Not a claim of completed work.

Watch an idea find its way.

A playful concept for seeing search algorithms think. Lay out a small world, add obstacles, and compare the paths each algorithm discovers.

## Exploration

Visualize breadth-first search, Dijkstra, and A* one step at a time, with explanations of their tradeoffs.
