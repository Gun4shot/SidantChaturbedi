# Contact artwork — built-in image generation

All assets were created with the built-in image tool, not the API/CLI fallback. Source PNGs are retained under `assets/source/`; runtime WebP exports preserve native dimensions and alpha. No image has been advertised as 4K or enlarged to imply additional detail.

## Veranda reference / static scene

Source: `assets/source/contact-veranda-v1.png`.

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: high-quality 2.5D matte painting for a scroll-driven portfolio finale. Reference image is the existing courtyard: preserve warm afternoon lighting, Himalayan setting, honey-colored stone and timber. Create the adjacent veranda reached by turning RIGHT around the courtyard tree, NOT the same frontal mountain view. Studio Ghibli inspired hand-drawn anime background, exceptionally crisp black ink contour lines around all architecture, plants, stones and props, vibrant emerald leaves, turquoise sky, warm amber timber, rich cel shading and delicately painted interiors. Maximum native detail, wide 3:2 landscape composition. Camera level at human eye height, approximately 60-degree horizontal FOV, vanishing horizon 60% down. Roof rafters frame top right, timber veranda posts at far edges, golden flagstone floor in bottom 35%, a low stone railing across back, terraced green hills and blue mountains visible left/back, climbing white and coral flowers on right. Large calm open area in CENTER for a separate letterbox/contact note to be composited later. No table, no box, no paper, no lettering, no people, no watermark. The left edge includes a broad dark-barked tree trunk continuing off the top and bottom-left edge, allowing a physical corner reveal. Make the image feel like a beautifully inked anime film background, not blurry watercolor, not photoreal, no depth of field or motion blur. Preserve sun direction from upper left.

## Continuous panorama draft

Source: `assets/source/contact-panorama-draft-v1.png`.

Use case: illustration-story / panoramic scene extension. Input 1 is the existing courtyard to preserve as LEFT HALF; input 2 is style/composition reference for its adjacent right veranda. Produce ONE seamless extra-wide 3:1 panorama, requested 3072x1024 or maximum native width. LEFT HALF must closely preserve input 1's complete courtyard composition: library roof far left, recognizable Himalayan peak slightly left of center of that half, low stone wall and open flagstone foreground, large courtyard tree at right edge of that half. Extend the world naturally to the RIGHT HALF by continuing the same stone floor, tree canopy, low wall and hillside into the sheltered veranda seen in input 2, with carved amber timber and climbing white/coral flowers at far right. This is ONE continuous 140-degree panoramic background, NOT a diptych, NOT two panels, NO vertical divider, NO seam, no repeated duplicate central mountain peak. The tree bridges the middle of the panorama naturally. Horizon consistent at about 60% canvas height; same level camera and warm sun from upper left across entire panorama. Studio Ghibli inspired crisp high-end ANIME background art with visibly BLACK INK OUTLINES around architecture, stone edges, leaves and silhouettes; rich vibrant emerald, turquoise and amber colors; clean cel shading plus finely painted detail. More defined and crisp than input 1, no soft oily smudges, no blur. Open flagstone ground along entire lower 35% and quieter space in center of RIGHT HALF to later place a letterbox. No box, no table, no people, no text, no watermark. Continuous geometric floor, coherent light, beautiful detailed hand-drawn anime panorama.

## Final panorama clean plate

Source: `assets/source/contact-panorama-v1.png`.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a CLEAN BACKGROUND PLATE from this exact panorama for animation. Remove ONLY the large central tree TRUNK, its large diagonal woody branches that connect to trunk, roots and raised planter/flowers surrounding its foot in the middle roughly 40–56% of image width. Fill those removed areas with a seamless continuation of the distant green hillside, mountain view, low stone railing and flagstone floor already visible immediately beside them. Keep the overhead leafy canopy as a framing canopy but without the bulky woody trunk blocking middle view. Preserve all other composition: left library, right veranda, all other flowers, mountains, sky, lighting, ink outlines, vibrant colors and 3:1 panoramic aspect ratio. No new objects. No other changes. We will overlay a separate transparent tree in code, so the central view must be clear. Maintain crisp black anime ink outlines and detailed Studio Ghibli inspired background painting. Export highest native resolution possible.

The initial standalone veranda was retained for the reduced-motion scene. The animated courtyard and contact share the final continuous panorama so there is no pair of unrelated paintings to splice during the turn. One clean-plate generation failed with a connection error; a retry using the same prompt succeeded. No CLI fallback was used.
